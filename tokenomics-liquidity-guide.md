## The Basics — Three Things That Matter

### 1. Price = Set by the pool ratio
When you deposit into the Aerodrome pool:
```
Price = USDC deposited ÷ SMESH deposited
```

- Deposit $500 USDC + 100,000 SMESH → **Price = $0.005** (target launch price)
- Deposit $500 USDC + 10,000 SMESH → **Price = $0.05** → $50M FDV
- Deposit $500 USDC + 1,000 SMESH → **Price = $0.50** → $500M FDV

You literally choose the market cap by choosing the ratio.

### 2. Market Cap = Price × Total Supply
SMESH has a **fixed supply of 1,000,000,000 tokens.**

| Price | FDV (Market Cap) |
|---|---|
| $0.005 | $5M |
| $0.01 | $10M |
| $0.05 | $50M |
| $0.10 | $100M |
| $0.50 | $500M |
| $1.00 | $1B |

FDV = Fully Diluted Valuation = what market cap would be if all 1B tokens circulated.

### 3. Liquidity = The Foundation Behind the Number
Market cap is just a number. Liquidity is what backs it.

**The hard truth:**
- You can set a $500M market cap with $500 in the pool
- But it falls apart the moment anyone sells even $50
- Market cap = billboard. Liquidity = foundation.

### Step 4: Buy Pressure × Price Appreciation = Pool Grows Itself

Example:

- Your token side of the LP is now worth ~$5,000
- Pool depth grows from $1K to ~$10K+ — automatically, from trading

The more people buy, the deeper the pool becomes. Self-reinforcing.

## How Aerodrome (SMESH's DEX) Works

Aerodrome uses the AMM model (x × y = k):
- Every trade moves the price
- The bigger the trade relative to pool size, the bigger the price move
- Adding LP doesn't change the price — just makes it more resilient

**Aerodrome specifics:**
- You choose pool type: stable (tight price range) or volatile
- Use **volatile pool** for SMESH/USDC (asset prices fluctuate freely)
- Aerodrome itself distributes AERO token rewards to LPs — extra incentive on top of yours
- Fees generated by trading are shared with LPs automatically

## Burn Mechanics — The Deflationary Edge

SMESH burns 5% of every transaction. This matters for long-term price:

- As volume grows, more SMESH is burned
- Fixed supply gets smaller over time
- Less supply at same demand = higher price
- At $1M/day volume → ~$50K worth of SMESH burned per day

At significant volume, burns become a meaningful price catalyst.
